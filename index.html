02 / How accounts scale

The Method

Scaling on Meta is a sequence. Skip a step and the algorithm punishes you with a learning reset or a CPL spike. This is the order I run it in, on every account.

STEP 01

Structure

Fewer campaigns, more signal

I collapse fragmented accounts into a lean structure so budget and conversion data pool in fewer places and Meta learns faster.

  • Consolidate overlapping campaigns and ad sets
  • Naming conventions anyone on the team can read
  • Clean exclusions and pixel events before a rupee is spent
STEP 02

Exit Learning

Get to stable delivery fast

Every ad set gets enough budget and a tight enough conversion event to leave the learning phase, so results stop swinging day to day.

  • Budget sized to the conversion volume Meta needs
  • No edits that trigger needless resets
  • Optimise for the event closest to revenue that still has volume
STEP 03

Creative Is Targeting

The hook picks the buyer

With broad audiences, the creative does the targeting. I write the hooks and copy myself and test angles, not colours.

  • Offer-first creative: payment plans, launch windows, price anchors
  • Hook tests in English, Hinglish and regional mixes
  • Fresh variants before fatigue shows up in frequency
STEP 04

Close The Loop

Optimise for sales, not form fills

Cheap leads that never pick up are expensive. I track leads through the CRM to site visits and purchases, then feed that back into what Meta optimises for.

  • Qualifying questions on lead forms
  • HubSpot handoff and weekly lead-quality reviews with sales
  • Weekly CPL, CTR and ROAS reporting in plain language
STEP 05

Scale

Grow spend without breaking it

Once cost per result holds, I push budget in measured steps and widen horizontally with proven creative, so spend grows and efficiency stays intact.

  • Stepped budget increases on winners
  • Horizontal scaling into new geographies and angles
  • Daily pacing against monthly targets

Reading an ad set at 2pm, knowing it won't recover, and killing it before it eats the day's budget. The value is in what you stop, not what you launch.

03 / What no course teaches

Field Notes

Five rules learned across 40+ real estate accounts and crores in monthly spend.

Rule 1

A cheap lead is not a good lead.

The lowest-CPL campaign is often not the one bringing people to site. Cost per site visit is the number. Everything else is a vanity metric with a rupee sign.

Rule 2

Averages hide bleeding ad sets.

Two strong ad sets can keep a campaign average looking fine while a third quietly burns budget. I read at ad-set level, every day.

Rule 3

One creative never fits two ticket sizes.

A ₹3 Cr sky villa buyer and a plot buyer want different things. Different hook, audience and format. Shared creative wastes budget fastest.

Rule 4

Dirty CRM data trains the algorithm wrong.

Optimise on bad data and Meta learns to find more of the wrong people. Fix the data before you fix the ads.

Rule 5

At scale, the job is allocation.

Running 14 accounts, the real weekly question was which account gets the next rupee. That call moved results more than any bid tweak.
